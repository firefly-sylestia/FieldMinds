Privacy Policy for FieldMind

Last Updated: January 19, 2026

Privacy-First Approach: FieldMind is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

1. Introduction

FieldMind ("we," "our," or "the app") is a free, open-source research and field note-taking tool for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data when you use it.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

FieldMind does not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any other personally identifiable information

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All app data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to external servers:

  • Field Notes & Observations: The app stores your research notes, observations, and other data on your device's local storage.
  • App Settings: Your preferences (theme, etc.) are saved locally
  • Imported/Exported Data: Data you import or export is stored only on your device

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

FieldMind may use internet connectivity for optional features. These features are disabled by default and require your explicit opt-in:

4.1 Optional Online Features (User Configurable)

  • Open-Meteo API: To fetch weather data (if enabled in settings)
  • Perenual API: To identify plant species from images (if enabled in settings)

4.2 Data Sent to Third-Party Services

When you enable online features, the following minimal data may be sent:

  • Location Data: Latitude and longitude for weather data lookup.
  • Image Data: Images sent for species identification.

Important: No personal information is included in these requests. The data is only used to enhance your research experience.

4.3 Third-Party Privacy Policies

When you use optional online features, the following third-party services may be contacted:

  • Open-Meteo: Open-Meteo Privacy Policy
  • Perenual: Perenual Privacy Policy

5. Permissions Used by the App

FieldMind requests the following Android permissions:

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: To access and attach media files to your notes.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To save exported data.
  • INTERNET: For optional online features.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To run background tasks like timers or data synchronization.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display notifications for reminders or background tasks.
  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep background tasks running.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: To get location for weather and observations.
  • CAMERA: To take photos and videos to attach to notes.

All permissions are used solely for app functionality and not for data collection.

6. Children's Privacy

FieldMind does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require any account creation or personal information.

7. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings. We recommend keeping your device updated and using lock screen protection.

8. Open Source and Transparency

FieldMind is fully open-source. You can review the entire source code on our GitHub repository.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the app and on our GitHub repository. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Your Rights

Since FieldMind does not collect any personal data, there is no data to:

  • Request access to
  • Request deletion of
  • Request correction of
  • Export or transfer

All your data remains under your control on your device.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, you can contact us via our GitHub repository.

12. Compliance

This app complies with:

  • Google Play Store Developer Program Policies
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

FieldMind is free and open-source software.
